Milisaka, Inc. is a rising corporation that provides software and electronics for military and corporate security. While our main focus is on obtaining government military contracts, Milisaka also offers several options for individuals and private organizations to purchase enterprise-grade cybersecurity solutions. Customers can place orders or request quotes for contracts via the company website.
